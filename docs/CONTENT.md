**Generation prompt:**

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: 16:9 homepage feature-section photograph for an Australian public-information website. Primary request: Create an original calm editorial lifestyle photograph of a blank deep-navy hard-cover notebook resting on a small stack of warm ivory books beside a handmade cream ceramic cup and a leafy indoor plant on a light neutral tabletop. Scene/backdrop: Quiet, uncluttered home reading or study setting with warm ivory linen and subtle natural material texture. Subject: The blank navy notebook is the visual focal point, supported by the ceramic cup and soft green plant leaves; no people or hands. Style/medium: Photorealistic natural editorial photography with authentic paper grain, ceramic imperfections, fabric texture, and restrained colour grading. Composition/framing: Wide 16:9 landscape crop, gently elevated three-quarter viewpoint, balanced for a web panel, with the notebook and cup fully visible near the centre-right and enough surrounding context to support responsive cropping. Lighting/mood: Soft diffused morning daylight, calm, reassuring, grounded and dignified; gentle natural shadows without dramatic contrast. Color palette: Deep navy, warm ivory, cream, muted natural green and light neutral timber. Constraints: The notebook and every book must be completely blank with no label, title, letters, numbers, symbols, logos, badges, seals or graphics. No people, no hands, no screens, no legal documents, no forms, no flags, no government insignia, no branded objects, no watermark. Avoid: readable or pseudo-readable text, typography, logos, trademarks, official-looking marks, overly polished product-advertising style, artificial HDR, harsh saturation, visual clutter, distress imagery.

The generated image contains no supplied third-party photograph. Publication approval and any organisation-wide policy for AI-generated imagery remain the responsibility of the project owner.
